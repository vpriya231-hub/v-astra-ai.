## Getting Started — BYOK Setup in 60 Seconds
Get V Astra AI running with your own API key in three steps:
**Step 1 — Get your API key**
> Visit [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) and generate a free Gemini API key. No billing required for standard usage tiers.
**Step 2 — Open V Astra AI settings**
> Navigate to **Settings → API Keys → Add Key**. Select your provider (e.g., Gemini) and paste your key.
**Step 3 — Start chatting**
> Return to the main chat screen. V Astra AI now runs entirely on your key — no subscription, no shared infrastructure. You're in control.

## Privacy & Permissions
V Astra AI requests only two permissions — both are optional and purpose-specific:

| Permission | Why it's needed | Data handling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🎙️ **Microphone** | Powers the Voice AI Assistant for hands-free queries | Audio processed in real-time; never stored or transmitted beyond your chosen AI provider |
| 📁 **Storage / Photos** | Enables AI Vision to analyze images and documents from your device | Files sent only to your configured AI provider on-demand; not retained by V Astra AI |

> **No account required. No behavioral tracking. No ads.**
> Your API key and notebooks remain on your device. V Astra AI does not operate its own AI backend — your data goes directly to the provider you configure.
